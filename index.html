Get started

1. Add a free Finnhub API key in Settings — needed for stocks only, crypto needs no key at all (CoinGecko's public API).

2. Add tickers in Stocks and coins in Crypto — AssetScope scores them automatically.

3. Use Analyzer any time to look up one stock or coin in depth, watchlisted or not.

AssetScope is a research and watchlist tool, not investment advice and not a trading platform. It never connects to a brokerage or exchange and never places, suggests placing, or is capable of placing a real order. Checklist scores are mechanical rule-of-thumb checks, not guarantees — verify everything yourself before making any financial decision. Crypto assets are especially volatile and speculative; scores here describe liquidity/momentum characteristics only, not a judgment on merit. Stock data via Finnhub.
